# Design review decisions — 2026-10-09

Status: architecture review completed; numerical and visual feasibility remain unmeasured. [design.md](../design.md) is the current specification. Its section 20 records unresolved experiments and their dependencies.

The second opinion exposed substantial weaknesses in sequencing, numerical policy, capacity planning, and reference interpretation. Those warranted changes. Several proposed conclusions were stronger than their evidence, and some suggested fixes would introduce new correctness problems if applied literally. The judgments below distinguish the criticism from the proposed remedy.

Evidence used: the existing design and source notes, native decoded reference frames (particularly 3.305 and 3.905 seconds), primary research listed in [the source map](../research/source-map.md), and explicit count/conditioning/timing calculations. No solver benchmark or new simulation was run for this review. Paper results and sizing calculations are not presented as measured browser feasibility.

## Reference and product judgments

| Review item | Judgment | Resulting decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Blue behind tan means three layers | **Visible omission valid; connectivity claim unproven.** Native frames contain a further blue depth surface. They do not prove whether it connects to another visible sheet outside the crop. | Author three independent tearable layers: blue, tan, blue. This is a scene reconstruction choice. Update contact, counts, picking, shadows, and captures consistently. |
| 2. Tan is thick brushed/felted material | **Edge criticism valid; felt identification too certain.** The tan edge is substantially denser and thicker; directional surface structure also remains visible. | Begin with thicker brushed woven backing, distinct fray/edge construction, and independent calibration. Do not adopt a nonwoven constitutive law from a fuzzy silhouette alone. A continuous-looking fiber band is allowed; an invented solid wall closing gaps is not. |
| 3. Denim dye/family structure must be explicit | **Substantially accept.** Traditional denim construction is a strong explanation of the blue/pale contrast and abrasion. The video cannot establish that every pale bridge is weft or that only warp wears. | Add warp-faced twill, surface-dyed warp with pale core, pale weft, radial dye coordinates, and exposure-dependent directional abrasion. Retain per-yarn variability and calibration. |
| 4. Sparse defects may never reach the reference | **Accept.** The original static-look and coupon gates did not explicitly prove reachable cellular damage. | Author an extensive correlated worn zone and add a multi-trajectory mechanical reachability gate. Track initial defects separately from newly generated holes; no prescribed crack sequence. |
| 13. Quarter-speed simulation is not low FPS | **Accept.** Continuous time dilation changes apparent acceleration and recoil. At time scale 0.25, apparent acceleration is scaled by 0.0625 when viewed against wall time. | Require natural-time steady operation. Benchmark timesteps by accuracy and cost. Use bounded buffering and independently certified interpolation; pause on exceptional overload rather than silently slowing everything. Persistent pauses fail. |
| 14. Reset versus minimal UI | **Retain reset.** Destructive interaction needs a discoverable restart; this is essential functionality. | One unobtrusive, accessible reset control remains. No additional panels or explanatory overlay. |

Three layers and unrestricted tearing throughout visible cloth are scope decisions made using the user's delegated judgment. They are not empirical solver decisions. The fixed worn region is a development specimen, not an invisible final damage boundary. Physical coupon dimensions and camera range remain provisional until matching the projected weave and reference composition.

## Mechanical and runtime architecture judgments

| Review item | Judgment | Resulting decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5. Compare IPC and block descent before committing | **Accept sequencing; reject inevitability and “cheap” equivalence.** Dependent reductions/dispatches make IPC a serious risk, but assumed microsecond costs and unrelated hard scenes cannot prove this scene impossible. OGC/contact/friction and AVBD state are not identical to IPC. | G1 performs bounded early comparisons at equal physical accuracy. Share fixtures and local constitutive definitions; account for method-specific work. Select one production solver after testing the chosen crossing representation with it. |
| 6. Displacement variables, stable energy differences, yarn-aware preconditioning | **Accept.** The previous numerical policy was too vague. The cited stiffness contrast is credible as a stress case. | Use displacement increments, stable local energy changes, structured reductions, generated local derivatives, and chain-aware preconditioning experiments. A banded chain solve is exact only for its chosen block; crossings and contact still couple the global system. |
| 7. Sliding crossings deserve early measurement | **Accept.** Persistent weave contact is a central cost, not a late polishing concern. | Compare general-contact rods and sliding-crossing coordinates early. Include release, material transport, general external collision, and transition work; do not assume switching to free rods is trivial. |
| 8. Fixed explicit region before promotion | **Accept as implementation sequence, not release scope.** The damaged initial material already requires explicit yarns. Fracture can be validated without solving stressed conversion first. | Gate order is G2 fixed equivalence → G4 fracture/reachability → G3 expansion. The fixed harness stops explicitly at its collar; a released product must expand or simulate the entire coupon explicitly. |
| 9. Remove routine CPU tear round trips | **Accept latency criticism; reject flag-only fracture and post-hoc safety validation.** Changing connectivity invalidates more than one segment record. | Precompile finite identities/sites and reserve topology capacity. GPU transactions update topology and validate before publication; CPU journals asynchronously. Prefer deterministic spans/pages before building a general GPU heap. Promotion still needs physical initialization/transfer, not just allocation. |
| 10. Fixed shell mesh before adaptivity | **Accept.** The supported coupon can first be tested with a converged fixed mesh. Adaptive transfer adds complexity without demonstrated necessity. | Use fixed 1–2 mm starting meshes and finer convergence comparisons. Runtime shell refinement is deferred unless measurements justify it. Vertex count alone does not establish sufficient fold/contact resolution. |
| 11. Linear midsurface coupling removes curved CCD | **Accept direction; qualify the proposed attachment.** Linear Cartesian coupling simplifies motion bounds. Projecting every crimped endpoint to zero offset can flatten yarn structure and distort bending. Two anchors are not, by themselves, an equivalence proof. | Exclude nonlinear normal-offset attachments from the first experiment. Test rest-compatible linear average constraints over complete weave-repeat collar windows, including moment transfer and mapped inertia. This remains a G2 hypothesis. Keep full-yarn mechanics as an alternative if it fails. |
| 12. Full promotion is guaranteed to fail at a 200k ceiling | **Capacity warning valid; stated guarantee invalid.** The original 200k value was a benchmark size, not a ceiling. Nevertheless, the gap to millions of potential nodes was insufficiently addressed. | Compile exact full-asset topology capacity and stress it in G1, then exercise actual destruction in G6. Use a provisional smaller finite coupon only if it still matches image scale/composition. Do not introduce a hidden explicit-area cap or pretend force limits bound accumulated damage. |

### Calculations that change implementation planning

- **Conditioning:** for `EA = 50–250 N`, segment length `0.0003 m`, node mass `9e-9 kg`, and step `1/240 s`, `EA/length = 1.67e5–8.33e5 N/m`, while `mass/h² = 5.184e-4 N/m`. Their ratio is approximately `3.2e8–1.6e9`. This is a local contrast, not the global matrix condition number or a measured material property.
- **Counts:** at 0.45 mm pitch, three 6×6 cm regions contain approximately 53,333 crossings; three 10×12.5 cm coupons contain approximately 185,185. An illustrative four nodes per crossing gives about 213k and 741k nodes, respectively, before extra process-site/subdivision reserves. Compile actual counts; do not promote these estimates into exact allocation constants.
- **Timing:** natural-time 240 Hz mechanics permits at most 4.17 ms per substep before rendering and other work. At 120 Hz it permits 8.33 ms. The previous 16.7 ms accounting applied to quarter-speed 240 Hz simulation and cannot support natural-time behavior. The acceptable timestep must come from temporal error tests.
- **Projected weave:** 0.45 mm pitch in a 10 cm field at 1,100 pixels spans approximately 4.95 pixels face-on and 0.86 pixels at 80° foreshortening. A single geometric pipeline is reasonable; “resolved everywhere” is not.

## Remaining review gaps

| Topic | Judgment and action |
| --- | --- |
| Storage bindings | Accept. SoA describes layout, not one binding per array. Specify arena offsets and a six-storage-binding initial compute plan within the portable eight-binding budget, plus bounded paging and cross-page gathers. |
| Named CCD and `f32` margin | Name ACCD for the IPC experiment and OGC bounds for the competing experiment. Do **not** invent one global epsilon before geometry kernels exist. Require error enclosures, uncertain-query handling, robust fallback frequency measurements, and a G1 stop condition. A safety factor alone is not a rounding-error guarantee. |
| Fuzz transparency/history | Accept the need for a concrete experiment. Start with stochastic coverage/four-sample MSAA; compare weighted blending if noise/cost fails. Specify disocclusion and mixed-layer history rejection. Final choice depends on moving pale-fray images, single-frame quality, and memory measurements in G0. |
| Visual shell/yarn seam | Accept. Add intact interface image/coverage/crimp/shadow comparison to G2, independently of force equivalence. |
| One intact rendering representation | Accept as baseline simplification, not as permission to omit filtering. Use shared geometric yarn reconstruction and retain footprint filtering at grazing views. Add a second aggregate cloth representation only if measured cost requires it and equivalence is demonstrated. |
| Single-source derivatives | Accept. Generate local energy derivatives for C++ and WGSL, with independent numerical and invariance checks. Shared code generation reduces drift but can share the same bug. |
| Missing sources | Accept. Add HYLC (R18), mechanics-aware yarn geometry (R19), procedural yarn fitting (R20), and OGC (R21), plus material-construction and coverage references (R22–R23). Distinguish source-supported methods from our integration hypotheses. |

OGC is a substantive candidate: the [author project](https://ankachan.github.io/Projects/OGC/) describes local feasibility bounds and includes yarn examples. The [IPC Toolkit release history](https://github.com/ipc-sim/ipc-toolkit/releases) also identifies an implementation and later feasibility fixes. This supports investigating it early; it does not select it as the winner, certify its WGSL port, or establish fracture-time correctness.

## What remains intentionally undecided

The production solver, crossing representation, precise interface constraints, safe finite-thickness rupture construction, kernel-specific collision tolerances, material parameters, final sampling/history method, and feasible full-scene timestep cannot honestly be settled by document review. Each now has an experiment, acceptance evidence, decision deadline, and blocked downstream work in design section 20.

The retained foundations are stable material identity, disjoint mass/energy ownership, cohesive fracture work, real load-bearing bridges, finite-patch grips, validated publication, deterministic diagnostics, and the pixel cap. The revision reduces unnecessary dependencies while keeping those invariants. It does not claim that the demanding integrated operating point has already been achieved.
